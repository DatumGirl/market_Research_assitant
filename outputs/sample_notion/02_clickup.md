# Competitor Briefing: ClickUp
_Prepared for: Notion · Generated: 2026-08-30 · Sources: 27_

## Snapshot
ClickUp is a San Diego-based "all-in-one" work management platform combining tasks, docs, goals, chat, whiteboards, dashboards, time tracking, and AI (ClickUp Brain) in a single workspace, positioned as a replacement for multiple point tools. It serves teams from freelancers to Fortune 500 companies, with reported scale of over 30,000 companies and 300,000 paid seats, 1K employees, and roughly $300M ARR as of 2026 at a $4B valuation (from its 2021 Series C).

## Pricing
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free Forever | $0 [^6] | Unlimited tasks and unlimited members, but capped storage (60MB shared storage), automations, and limited view types |
| Unlimited | $7/user/month billed annually (~$10/user/month billed monthly) [^3] | Unlimited custom views, timesheets, guest access, 100 mind map creations |
| Business | $12/user/month billed yearly ($19/user/month billed monthly) [^1] | Private docs, sprint reporting, all dashboard views, 25,000 automation actions/month |
| Business Plus | Not listed with public price; pulled from public marketing page [^6] | ~50,000 automations per month; in-app upgrade path only, no longer publicly advertised |
| Enterprise | Custom quote [^3] | SAML SSO, advanced permissions, audit logs, dedicated success manager, white labelling, higher automation ceiling (250,000 runs/month) |

## Core Features
- **Converged workspace (tasks, docs, goals, chat, whiteboards, dashboards)** — Directly competes with Notion's all-in-one positioning by claiming natively connected modules rather than integrated point products, reducing app-switching for prospects evaluating both tools. [^12]
- **ClickUp Brain / Everything AI (context-aware AI assistant)** — AI is monetized as a separate per-seat add-on ($9-$28/user/month), giving ClickUp an AI upsell lever that Notion must match or undercut to stay competitive on total cost. [^6]
- **15+ view types (Gantt, board, list, whiteboards, dashboards)** — Appeals to teams with complex, varied workflows (PM, ops, engineering) — a broader visualization set than Notion's more document-centric view model. [^7]
- **Custom Roles and Permissions** — Enables ClickUp to serve enterprise buyers needing granular access control, an area where Notion has historically been perceived as weaker for complex org structures. [^7]
- **1,000+ native integrations plus Zapier** — Reduces switching friction for teams with existing tool stacks, strengthening ClickUp's 'replace your stack' pitch against Notion's more limited integration surface. [^7]
- **Automations with tiered usage caps (25,000-250,000 actions/month)** — Creates a built-in upgrade trigger (mid-contract expansion revenue) that also signals ClickUp's deeper workflow-automation focus versus Notion's simpler automation set. [^5]
- **Time tracking and sprint/reporting tools** — Targets project-management-heavy teams (agile, agencies) with native PM depth that Notion typically covers via third-party integrations. [^11]

## Market Positioning
- **Segment:** All-in-one work/project management platform spanning SMB to enterprise, explicitly branded as 'The Everything App for work'
- **Ideal customer:** Cross-functional teams with complex workflows — project managers, ops, marketing, and creative teams at companies in the 'messy middle' of growth (per bloomberry data, common company sizes 0-9, 20-49, and 100-249 employees)
- **Differentiation:** Claims to be a 'converged' workspace where tasks, docs, chat, and goals are natively connected (not just integrated), replacing multiple point tools and reducing app-switching costs; heavier emphasis on PM-specific depth (Gantt, sprints, automations) than document-first competitors
- **Geography:** Global, with 56.34% of project-collaboration customers in the United States; international expansion into Europe/Australia (Dublin, Sydney offices) was funded post-Series C
- **Go-to-market:** Freemium/viral top-of-funnel (Free Forever plan) converting self-serve users to paid, combined with targeted demand-gen and direct enterprise sales; described as achieving 80% YoY revenue growth via humor-driven brand campaigns and rapid weekly product releases

## Strategic Implications for Notion
- **Threat:** ClickUp's breadth of native PM features (Gantt, sprints, automations, time tracking) gives it a stronger pitch than Notion for teams needing structured project management, not just docs/wiki collaboration.
- **Threat:** Aggressive freemium-to-paid conversion (reported 60% of free users converting) and viral growth engine could continue capturing SMB and mid-market teams before they evaluate Notion.
- **Threat:** ClickUp explicitly positions against Notion by name in its marketing/GTM messaging, indicating direct competitive targeting rather than incidental overlap.
- **Threat:** Rapid weekly release cadence and $300M ARR scale suggest ClickUp has resources to close feature gaps (e.g., AI, docs) faster than smaller competitors.
- **Opportunity:** ClickUp's AI is a paid add-on layered on top of workspace fees ($9-$28/user/month extra), creating a total-cost-of-ownership vulnerability Notion can exploit if its own AI is bundled or priced more transparently.
- **Opportunity:** ClickUp's removal of Business Plus from the public pricing page and general pricing complexity (hidden costs, automation overages per Vendr data) creates a 'confusing pricing' narrative Notion could contrast against with simpler tiering.
- **Opportunity:** ClickUp's 'everything app' positioning risks feature bloat/complexity; Notion can target teams seeking a cleaner, more flexible docs-first experience as counter-positioning.
- **Opportunity:** Automation action caps forcing mid-contract upgrades (15-25% cost increases per Vendr) suggest customer friction points Notion could highlight in competitive displacement campaigns.
